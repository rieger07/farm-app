FARM & LIVESTOCK OPERATIONS

From hatch or breeding to freezer & market.

Homestead Planner is a web application built and operated by Ironclad Systems LLC to help small farms and homesteads schedule livestock batches, forecast feed needs, track losses and dressed yields, and organize farm expense receipts.

Sign in to Homestead Planner → Request access / Contact

Batch & Milestone Radar

Track poultry chicks, egg incubation, and mammal breeding lifecycles on a visual timeline with 14-day milestone reminders and .ics / Google Calendar links.

Feed & Yield Forecasting

Plan starter and grower bag purchases, log actual feed purchases by date, record mortality and dressed harvest weights, and compare planned vs. actual cost per pound.

Schedule F Expenses

Record feed, processing, supplies, and equipment expenses by category, attach receipts, and export a clean Schedule F CSV for bookkeeping.

Optional Google Drive Receipt Integration & Data Transparency

Homestead Planner works without connecting Google. If you choose to connect your Google account in Settings → Google Drive Receipts, we request only two minimal scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file — Used solely to create your chosen receipt folder (for example, Homestead Planner Receipts) and upload receipt photos or PDFs you explicitly submit from batch or expense forms. Homestead Planner cannot view, modify, or delete any other files in your Google Drive.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email — Used solely to display which Google account is currently connected in your Settings page.

Receipt files remain in your own Google Drive account. OAuth tokens are stored encrypted solely to perform uploads you initiate, are never sold or shared with third parties, and can be disconnected at any time in Settings. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Privacy Policy — Homestead Planner

Operated by Ironclad Systems LLC · Effective November 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use Homestead Planner, we store the account details you provide (username, display name, and hashed password) and the farm operational records you enter (livestock batches, species presets, feed logs, losses, harvests, sales/freezer allocations, and expense metadata).

2. Google User Data & Google Drive Integration

Connecting Google Drive is completely optional. If you authorize Homestead Planner to connect to your Google account, we request:

  • Google Drive (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file): Used exclusively to create a designated receipt folder in your Google Drive and upload receipt photos or PDFs that you choose to attach to feed, harvest, or farm expense entries. Homestead Planner only accesses files and folders created by Homestead Planner and never reads or modifies other files in your Drive.
  • Google Email (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email): Used exclusively to show your connected Google email address in Settings.

OAuth access and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest and used solely to perform uploads you initiate. We do not sell, rent, or transfer Google user data to third parties, and we do not use Google user data for advertising or AI/ML model training.

Homestead Planner's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Data Retention & Revoking Access

You can disconnect Google Drive at any time from Settings → Google Drive Receipts → Disconnect, which deletes your stored OAuth tokens from our database while leaving your uploaded receipt files intact in your Google Drive. You may also revoke access from your Google Account security settings, or export/delete your Homestead Planner data at any time.

4. Contact

Questions or data deletion requests can be sent via ironcladsystems.dev/contact.

Privacy Policy

Ironclad Systems LLC ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy describes how we handle information on ironcladsystems.dev and our hosted applications including Iron Ledger.

Information we collect

When you sign up for an account or contact us, we collect your business/workspace name, username, display name, and billing email. Payment card details are processed directly by Stripe and are never stored on our servers.

Third-party integrations

If you connect a Zettle POS account to Iron Ledger, OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest and used solely to synchronize your product catalog and purchase history into your workspace.

Data usage & deletion

We do not sell or share your business data with third parties. To request data export or deletion, reach out via our contact form.
